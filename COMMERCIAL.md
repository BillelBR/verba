## How to get one

Open an issue titled "Commercial license" at https://github.com/BillelBR/verba/issues (do not put private information in it) or write to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile `BillelBR`. Say what the organisation is and how many people would use Verba.

The terms (price, duration, number of computers) are agreed in writing between the maintainer and the organisation. There is no public price list yet.
