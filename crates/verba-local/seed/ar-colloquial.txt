# Spoken Arabic written in chat -> Modern Standard Arabic, for sentence translation from Arabic.
#
# The translation network (OPUS-MT ar-en) was trained on written Modern Standard Arabic. It turns a Darija word into a name or noise
# ("دروك" became "Drake", "راني" became "Rani"), and it translated the Levantine "زلمة" (man, mate) into a slur. Before a sentence goes to
# the network, the words below are replaced by their Modern Standard Arabic form, so that the sentence is one the network knows.
#
# Format:   spellings | modern standard Arabic
#   - spellings are separated by commas; a spelling may be two or three words ("واش راك": the longest match wins);
#   - the match is on whole words, ignoring short vowels, hamza and alef forms, and the Algerian letter ڨ counts as ق;
#   - what is not in this list is left as it is.
#
# NOT REVIEWED by an Algerian or other native speaker: written from general knowledge of Algerian Darija (most lines) and of the
# Levantine, Gulf and Egyptian words that come up most in chat. Where a word can mean two things the commonest meaning is taken.
# Words that mean something else in Modern Standard Arabic or are names (دراهم, وليد, صحة, غالي) and words that cannot be written out as one
# Modern Standard word in every context ("واش" alone) are left out on purpose.
