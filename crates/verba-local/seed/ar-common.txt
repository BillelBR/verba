# Common Modern Standard Arabic words and their English translations, written for Verba (not taken from a corpus or a dictionary), for the words
# that the word lists or the Wiktionary dictionary were found to lack (the build of this file's useful part is measured in docs/ACCURACY.md).
# One line: the Arabic word (or short phrase), a TAB, the English translations separated by `;` (most common first). Arabic without short vowels.
# What the program does with it (crates/verba-local/src/lib.rs, translate.rs):
#  - the dictionary: `ar>en` gets the line when the headword is missing, or its missing translations at the END (nothing is reordered);
#    `en>ar` gets the Arabic word at the END of each one-word English translation (a phrase is not reversed);
#  - the suggestions: a one-word Arabic headword that the Arabic list lacks is added to it, as a word of ordinary frequency.
# Written from general knowledge; an Arabic speaker should read it (a wrong line here is a wrong translation shown to people).
