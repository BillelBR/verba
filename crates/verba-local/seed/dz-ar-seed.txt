# Verba - Algerian Darija written in ARABIC script: SEED word list.
#
# Written by the Verba authors from their own knowledge of how Algerians write Darija in Arabic letters. It is NOT taken from any corpus.
# License: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (same as the code). It is an estimate, not a measurement: see docs/ACCURACY.md.
#
# Format (read by crates/verba-local/src/dz.rs, same as dz-seed.txt):
#   - lines starting with '#' are comments;
#   - a word is written without short vowels (no harakat), without spaces, in Arabic letters (also ڨ ڤ پ چ گ);
#   - "word 500" gives the word its weight (about how often it is written, relative to the other words of this file);
#   - a word with no number takes the weight of the last "@N" line (until the next "@N");
#   - spelling variants are separate words (there is no standard spelling): the commonest variant gets the highest weight.
#     Hamza on alef, ta marbuta / ha, ya / alef maqsura, and ڨ / ق, ث / ت, ذ / د, ظ / ض count as the same letter when words are compared
#     (see `DzList::key`), so one spelling is enough for them; the variants listed here are the ones that differ in more than that.
#
# Everyday words that Modern Standard Arabic shares (family, food, the days, religious phrases) are in the list too: Algerians write them in Darija
# texts and their spellings and weights differ; the program offers them only where they beat the Arabic list (see docs/ACCURACY.md).
# Vulgar words are left out on purpose.
# The last section ("UNSURE") holds words and spellings the authors are not certain are Algerian (they may be Moroccan, Tunisian,
# Egyptian or Levantine, or rare): the Algerian reviewer decides.
