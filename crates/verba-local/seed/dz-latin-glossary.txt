# Verba - Algerian Darija written in LATIN letters: a small glossary for word translation (Darija -> English, French, Arabic).
#
# Written by the Verba authors from their own general knowledge of how Algerians write and what the words mean. It is NOT taken from a
# corpus and it has NOT been checked by an Algerian reviewer: the reviewer should read it (see docs/ACCURACY.md).
# License: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (same as the code).
#
# Format: one entry per line, four fields separated by "|":
#     spellings (comma separated) | English | French | Arabic
#   - "; " separates several translations inside a field, most common first; "-" means no translation in that language.
#   - the spellings are the ones people write (digits stand for letters: 3 = ع, 7 = ح, 9 = ق, 5 = خ). A spelling that is not listed is
#     still found when it differs only in doubled letters or in vowels (bezzaf, bzaf, bezaf), unless that would be ambiguous.
#   - a word that English or French knows is translated as English or French first (a Latin word is looked up there before here), unless
#     the spelling starts with "!": then it is Darija first (wesh, ana, dima ...).
