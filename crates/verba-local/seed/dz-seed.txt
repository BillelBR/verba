# Format (read by crates/verba-local/src/dz.rs):
#   - lines starting with '#' are comments;
#   - a word is lower case: letters a-z and the digit-letters 2 3 5 6 7 8 9
#     (3 = ain, 7 = h, 9 = q, 5 = kh, 8 = gh, 6 = t, 2 = hamza);
#   - "word 500" gives the word its weight (about how often it is typed, relative to the other words of this file);
#   - a word with no number takes the weight of the last "@N" line (until the next "@N");
#   - spelling variants are separate words, there is no standard spelling: the commonest variant gets the highest weight.
#
# Vulgar words are left out on purpose: a suggestion popup must not offer them.
# The last section ("UNSURE") holds words and spellings the authors are not certain of: the Algerian reviewer decides.
